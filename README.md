# satellite  💍
**Jewelry Personal Styling & Online Select Shop**

## Overview
**satellite** is a jewelry personal styling brand and online select shop designed to reduce  
decision fatigue in online jewelry shopping experiences.

To address the difficulty of perceiving sensory qualities—such as thickness, ornamentation,  
and overall mood—satellite introduces a **Typology System** that helps users clearly  
articulate their preferences and make confident selections based on consistent criteria.

## Role
- Brand Identity & Experience Design  
- UX·UI Design (Web / App)  
- Web Publishing (Desktop Demo)  
- Packaging Design  

> This was a personal project, fully designed and developed independently.

## Key Design Focus
- **Typology System**  
  Jewelry styles are structured across two axes—*Fancy–Simple* and *Thin–Bold*—  
  enabling intuitive discovery and comparison.
- **Consistent Brand Experience**  
  Brand identity, digital interfaces, and packaging were designed as a unified system.
- **Decision Fatigue Reduction**  
  The experience prioritizes clarity and confidence throughout the selection journey.
